SPECTIARY DATA SOURCES

This file covers third-party data incorporated into the built-in public spectral-line catalog. The catalog is provided for visual reference and is not intended for wavelength calibration or authoritative quantitative analysis.

The Atomic Line List website states that all data distributed through its webpages are licensed under CC BY 4.0. Spectiary uses a selected subset of its vacuum wavelengths for H I, C I, C II, Li I, Na I, Mg I, K I, Ca I, and Ca II. Spectiary selected the rows, assigned application-specific identifiers and labels, and rounded wavelengths to 0.001 Angstrom.

The Li I 6708 catalog marker combines the AtLL transitions at 6709.613 and 6709.764 Angstrom into one unresolved band marker. The K I catalog markers use the AtLL transitions at 7667.021 and 7701.093 Angstrom as separate lines. The Mg I 8809 catalog marker uses the AtLL transition at 8809.175 Angstrom.

The carbon catalog markers retain isolated AtLL transitions as line markers and combine only sub-Angstrom components intended as one low-resolution visual feature. The combined C I multiplet markers contain the following vacuum components: 6014.831 and 6014.878 Angstrom for C I 6015, and 7117.134 and 7117.144 Angstrom for C I 7117. The combined C II multiplet markers contain the following vacuum components: 4075.631, 4075.694, and 4075.991 Angstrom for C II 4076; 4268.202, 4268.384, and 4268.462 Angstrom for C II 4268; 4373.604, 4373.730, and 4373.743 Angstrom for C II 4374; 4619.853 and 4620.543 Angstrom for C II 4620; 6152.968 and 6153.237 Angstrom for C II 6153; and 6463.736 and 6463.915 Angstrom for C II 6464. These ranges are display markers for line identification and are not wavelength-calibration intervals.

AtLL v3.00b5 is a beta release offered for testing and may change without notice. AtLL states that its wavelengths are calculated from differences between transition energy levels, except for weighted hydrogenic lines, and that observed wavelengths have only limited coverage. Spectiary therefore uses these values for visual line identification, not as wavelength-calibration truth.

Requested scientific citation:
P. A. M. van Hoof (2018), "A New Atomic Line List",
Galaxies 6(2), 63. https://doi.org/10.3390/galaxies6020063

Ba II measured wavelengths
==============================================================================

Source:
C. Ferrara, M. Giarrusso, and F. Leone (2024),
"Experimental atomic data of spectral lines - I. Cs, Ba, Pr, Nd, Sm, Eu, Gd,
Tb, Dy, Ho, Er, Tm, Yb, Lu, Hf, Re, and Os in the 370-1000 nm interval",
Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society 527(3), 4440-4466.
https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stad3230

Copyright: The Author(s), 2023
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Spectiary uses the observed Ba II wavelengths reported as 4554.024 Angstrom and 6496.891 Angstrom. Spectiary converted the reported visible air wavelengths to vacuum using the refractive-index relation documented by Morton (2000), equation 3, then rounded the results to 0.001 Angstrom.

D. C. Morton (2000), "Atomic Data for Resonance Absorption Lines. II.
Wavelengths Longward of the Lyman Limit for Heavy Elements",
The Astrophysical Journal Supplement Series 130, 403.
https://doi.org/10.1086/317349

Spectiary-curated approximate markers
==============================================================================

Markers tagged in the former TSV by the source handle "spectiary_curated_approximate" are rounded visual annotations curated by Spectiary rather than redistributed precision measurements. Sr II 4078 is stored as an approximate 4078.9 Angstrom vacuum marker. It is not intended for wavelength calibration.

Other markers tagged in the former TSV by the historical handle "carbon_star_requirements" are approximate molecular or feature markers originating in the public Spectiary requirements catalog. They are likewise intended only for visual annotation.

The canonical JSON conversion preserves the marker IDs and scientific values of these records. Source handles are historical transformation metadata retained here, not fields in ordinary Spectral Line List v1 documents.
