## Interpretation and next diagnostic boundary

The longest observed stalls are inside the existing native platform-size callback,
before the renderer's buffer rebuild. Main draw submission, explicit available
waits, and renderer buffer rebuilding do not explain those particular long frames.
Buffer reset and detached Present still add measurable cost to ordinary resize
frames; this is not a claim that those costs are negligible or that one change
will solve every pacing problem.

The locally available Dear ImGui 1.92.8 Win32 backend source implements
`ImGui_ImplWin32_SetWindowSize` with client-to-window rect adjustment followed by
`SetWindowPos(..., SWP_NOZORDER | SWP_NOMOVE | SWP_NOACTIVATE)`
(`build/ninja-msvc-debug/_deps/specforge_widget_imgui_source-src/backends/imgui_impl_win32.cpp:1341`).
Its platform update invokes Platform_SetWindowSize before Renderer_SetWindowSize
(`imgui.cpp:17525`). The production installed header also reports version 1.92.8.
This source inspection identifies the next area to investigate, but timing the
whole callback does not separately time its individual Win32 calls.

Remaining uncertainty: rect adjustment versus SetWindowPos and any synchronous
messages, driver/compositor interaction or thread descheduling within that call.
No stack trace or GPU/DWM wait attribution is present. Thus the evidence localizes
the long-stall call boundary, but does not prove a specific OS/driver mechanism.
Investigate that callback with narrower call timings or a stack-enabled Windows
performance trace before choosing window-size flags, deferral, buffer strategy,
or presentation changes. No such policy changes are made by this analysis.
