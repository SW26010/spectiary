Local helper scripts are `logs/extract-resize-etl.ps1`,

TraceEvent's `CSwitchTraceData.BlockingStack()` via TraceLog, not the ordinary
event `CallStack()` (which can belong to the incoming thread). Raw exact-timestamp
StackWalk matching alone did not recover these blocking stacks. Conversion logs
also retain unrelated unresolved-stack/image warnings; the selected blocking
stacks above were resolved separately.

## Next decision

The evidence justifies investigating the detached Composition HWND's redirection
surface configuration. The inspected ImGui Win32 backend creates detached HWNDs
with APPWINDOW/TOOLWINDOW and optional TOPMOST styles, without
`WS_EX_NOREDIRECTIONBITMAP`. Microsoft documents this latter style as a way to
[avoid a window redirection surface](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/archive/msdn-magazine/2014/june/windows-with-c-high-performance-window-layering-using-the-windows-composition-engine).
That makes it a candidate for a separate controlled experiment, not an approved
or proven fix. Compatibility with window creation/update, native decorations,
docking, Composition failure and DXGI fallback must be considered before changing
the HWND contract. It must not be silently added to this instrumentation change.

Unknowns remain: the fence's producer/completion dependency and the reason for
its latency, whether avoiding this redirection surface eliminates the long tail,
and whether that change preserves all supported presentation paths. These logs
do not establish a vendor-driver defect or explain every main-window behavior.
No further identical capture is needed to establish the direct blocking chain.
